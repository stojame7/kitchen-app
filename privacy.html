Privacy policy

How MenuWeave handles your data

Last updated: 28 September 2026

The short version

  • We collect your name, email, country and meal preferences so we can save your timetables.
  • We only send update emails if you ask for them, and you can stop them anytime.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the account menu.

1. Who we are

MenuWeave is a meal-planning app available at stojame7.github.io/kitchen-app. MenuWeave is responsible for (the "data controller" of) the personal data described here. You can contact us about privacy at [your contact email].

3. Our legal basis

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we process your data because it is necessary to provide the service you signed up for (your account and timetables), and on the basis of your consent for update emails and for faith-related preferences you choose to give. You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your data. We use a few service providers to run MenuWeave:

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved timetables and handles sign-in and account emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app. Like any web host, it may log your IP address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the typeface and the tools used for downloads. They receive your IP address when your browser loads these files.
  • [Your email tool, e.g. Brevo, if you use one] sends update emails to people who opted in.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

5. Where your data is stored

Our providers may store and process data outside Nigeria, including in [your Supabase region, e.g. the European Union]. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards the Nigeria Data Protection Act allows for international transfers, such as the providers' contractual data protection commitments.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your profile and saved timetables are deleted straight away. Backups held by our providers are overwritten within their normal backup cycle. If an account has not been used for 24 months, we may delete it after emailing you first.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your account and data (you can do this yourself: account menu → Delete my account);
  • object to or restrict how we use your data;
  • stop update emails (account menu, or the unsubscribe link in any email);
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng) if you're unhappy with how we handle your data.

To use any of these rights, email [your contact email]. We'll reply within 30 days.

8. Storage on your device

MenuWeave saves your theme choice and your latest timetable in your browser's local storage so the app opens quickly, and our sign-in provider stores a sign-in token there to keep you logged in. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Signing out removes your timetable from the device.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules ensure each account can only read and change its own records. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we'll notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

10. Children

MenuWeave is meant for adults planning meals for themselves or their households. People under 18 should only create an account with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has signed up without it, contact us and we'll delete the account.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app or by email.
